Privacy Policy

Effective Date: March 20, 2026

Welcome to Levian AI (accessible at levianai.qzz.io). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any basic information you share with us when using our platform.

1. Information We Collect

When you choose to sign in using Google Sign-In, we only collect and access non-sensitive account profile details required for identification and session sync:

  • Google Account Email Address: Used to securely isolate and identify your saved chat sessions.
  • Profile Name & Picture: Used solely to display your user profile state within the application interface.
  • Chat History Data: Conversations and prompt inputs stored locally or synced via your account state to provide session continuity.

2. How We Use Your Information

The minimal data we retrieve is used strictly for core app functionality:

  • To authenticate your identity and maintain your active session.
  • To sync and retrieve your saved conversational history across reloads.
  • We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your personal information or chat logs with any third parties.

3. Data Storage and Security

Your session data and chat logs are stored locally on your device via browser storage (`localStorage`) and/or securely synchronized under your designated user account. We employ standard protective practices to ensure safe handling of your credentials.

4. Third-Party Services

We utilize Google Identity Services for user authentication. Your interaction with Google Sign-In is subject to Google's respective privacy policies and terms of service.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted directly on this page with an updated effective date.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, you can reach out via the support email at support@novatech.qzz.io or else visit the main page at novatech.qzz.io.
